
Steps in Experiment
Step 1: Weigh Brass Sample
Active Variable: weight (Slider range: 1.0 - 2.0 g, default 1.5 g)
User Interaction: Select the weight in the variables panel, then click the Start button.
Animation: A spatula moves from its rest position to the brass sample dish, scoops up the brass foil, moves over to the beaker, tilts to drop the foil into the beaker, and returns to its rest position.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 2: Add 1:1 Nitric Acid ($HNO_3$)
Active Variable: hno3 (Slider range: 5.0 - 15.0 mL, valid range is 8.0 - 12.0 mL)
User Interaction: Set the volume slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: A pipette moves to the $HNO_3$ reagent bottle, draws the acid (visible liquid rising in the pipette), moves above the beaker, dispenses a stream of liquid causing the beaker's liquid level to rise, and returns to its rest position.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 3: Dissolution Reaction (Automatic)
Active Variable: None (Controls are disabled)
User Interaction: Starts automatically after hitting Next from Step 2.
Animation: Brown nitrogen dioxide ($NO_2$) gas particles rise from the beaker. The liquid in the beaker turns from clear to green as the brass sample dissolves, and the brass foil pieces shrink.
To Proceed: Wait for the animation to finish, then click the Next button.

Step 4: Expel Nitrogen Oxides / Boil (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Starts automatically after hitting Next from Step 3.
Animation: The heating plate glows red. The beaker's liquid color transitions to blue-green as the reaction completes, any remaining foil pieces dissolve fully, and the brown gas particles fade away.
To Proceed: Wait for the heating animation to finish, then click the Next button.

Step 5: Add Urea
Active Variable: urea (Slider range: 0.5 - 1.5 g, valid range is 0.8 - 1.2 g)
User Interaction: Set the urea weight slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The spatula scoops urea powder, carries it over to the beaker, tilts to drop it in, and returns. The liquid level in the beaker rises slightly.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 6: Cool & Dilute
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Click the Play button.
Animation: A wash bottle moves over to the beaker, tilts, and pours a stream of distilled water into the beaker before returning. The beaker's liquid level increases.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 7: Transfer & Pipette (Two-part Animation)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Click the Play button.
Animation:
Transfer: The beaker rises, tilts, and pours the blue-green solution into a 250 mL volumetric flask, causing the flask's liquid level to rise. The beaker returns to the table.
Pipette: A pipette descends into the volumetric flask, draws 25 mL of the diluted solution, transfers it over to a clean conical flask, dispenses it, and returns.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 8: Add Ammonium Hydroxide ($NH_4OH$)
Active Variable: nh4oh (Slider range: 5.0 - 15.0 mL, valid range is 8.0 - 12.0 mL)
User Interaction: Set the volume slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The pipette draws ammonium hydroxide from the reagent bottle and dispenses it dropwise into the conical flask.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 9: Precipitate Formed (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Starts automatically after hitting Next from Step 8.
Animation: The solution in the conical flask turns cloudy and develops a pale bluish-white precipitate of copper hydroxide ($Cu(OH)_2$).
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 10: Add Acetic Acid ($CH_3COOH$)
Active Variable: acetic (Slider range: 2.0 - 8.0 mL, valid range is 4.0 - 6.0 mL)
User Interaction: Set the volume slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The pipette draws acetic acid from the reagent bottle and dispenses it into the conical flask.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 11: Dissolve Precipitate (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Starts automatically after hitting Next from Step 10.
Animation: The pale bluish-white precipitate dissolves, and the liquid in the conical flask becomes a clear, royal blue copper complex solution.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 12: Add Potassium Iodide ($KI$)
Active Variable: ki (Slider range: 5.0 - 15.0 mL, valid range is 8.0 - 12.0 mL)
User Interaction: Set the volume slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The pipette draws $KI$ from the reagent bottle and dispenses it into the conical flask.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 13: Iodine Liberated (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Starts automatically after hitting Next from Step 12.
Animation: The solution in the conical flask turns dark brown, representing the liberated iodine suspension.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 14: Start Titration
Active Variable: thiosulfate (Volume added from burette)
User Interaction: Adjust the thiosulfate slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The stopcock of the burette rotates, and drips of thiosulfate fall into the conical flask. The liquid level in the conical flask rises while the level in the burette drops.
To Proceed: Click the Next button (this becomes active when approaching the first endpoint color change).

Step 15: Near Endpoint / Pale Yellow (Automatic Transition)
Active Variable: thiosulfate
User Interaction: Triggered automatically by the titration progress.
Animation: The dark brown suspension in the conical flask fades to a pale yellow color.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 16: Add Starch Indicator
Active Variable: starch (Slider range: 1.0 - 3.0 mL, valid range is 1.5 - 2.5 mL)
User Interaction: Set the volume slider, then click the Play button.
Animation: The pipette draws starch from the indicator bottle and dispenses it into the conical flask.
To Proceed: The simulation will automatically transition to Step 17 as soon as this animation finishes.

Step 17: Deep Blue Complex (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Starts automatically after Step 16's pipette leaves the flask.
Animation: The pale yellow liquid in the conical flask turns into a deep, dark purplish-blue starch-iodine complex.
To Proceed: Click the Next button.

Step 18: Continue Titration
Active Variable: thiosulfate
User Interaction: Carefully adjust the thiosulfate slider dropwise, then click the Play button.
Animation: Drips fall from the burette, and the blue color begins to fade.
To Proceed: Click the Next button when the endpoint color change is close.

Step 19: Color Fading
Active Variable: thiosulfate
User Interaction: Slide the thiosulfate volume up to the exact endpoint (concordant value) and click Next.
Animation: The blue color fades away, indicating the titration is complete.
To Proceed: If the value added matches the required endpoint volume within $\pm0.1$ mL, clicking Next will successfully complete the trial.

Step 20: Titration Complete / Milky White (Automatic)
Active Variable: None
User Interaction: Final step of the trial.
Animation: The solution becomes a milky-white suspension due to the cuprous iodide ($CuI$) precipitate. The final titration volume is saved, the observation table is updated, and chemical calculations are computed in the results panel.
Next Trial: A button appears to allow you to start Trial 2 or Trial 3.
